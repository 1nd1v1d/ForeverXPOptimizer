# Summary

See which XP boosts you are missing at your level and get buff reminders, with active XP tracking and a quest turn-in preview.

# Description

**Make every XP boost count.**

Forever XP Optimizer puts your **XP boosters at the center of your leveling journey**. See which bonuses are active, what you are missing and which sources become available as you level, alongside a compact XP tracker.

**Keep your XP boosts in view.** Monitor XP food, Cozy Sleeping Bag stacks, rested experience and camping opportunities. Get reminders when relevant buffs are missing, incomplete or about to expire, with details explaining the items and requirements involved.

- **XP food:** Check your buff status and suitable food in your bags.
- **Cozy Sleeping Bag:** Track stacks, remaining buff time and item cooldown.
- **Rested XP:** See how much bonus experience remains in your reserve.
- **Camp Tent:** Identify opportunities to replenish rested experience.
- **Situational bonuses:** Keep relevant items, such as the undead XP pendant, on your radar.
- **Level-based guidance:** Explore sources for your current level or preview later milestones.

Each source can be switched off individually, so your reminders match how you want to play.

**Know how fast you are progressing.** The integrated tracker shows active XP per hour, estimated time to your next level, total character playtime, session time and recorded level history. AFK time is displayed separately and excluded from your active leveling rate. A manual pause gives you control over other breaks.

**See what your completed quests are worth.** A golden overlay previews the known XP waiting in quests ready to turn in. See their combined reward and the projected level and progress after handing them in, including multiple level-ups. Hover over the bar for individual quest rewards. Pending quest XP only enters your measured XP/hour after you actually earn it.

**Your client language, automatically.** The interface follows your WoW client's text-language setting. Includes English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. Regional variants share their base translation; English is the fallback for unsupported languages or missing text. No additional addons or online translation services are required. Your saved settings and level history stay intact when switching languages.

**Keep the display your way.** Move and scale the compact bar, expand the booster overview and level history, adjust expiration warnings and enable an optional notification sound.

Type **`/fxp`** to open the display. Right-click it to expand the details. **`/fxp help`** lists commands in your client language; the English commands work in every language.

**Compatibility:** World of Warcraft: Forever 1.60.1, interface 16001. Addon version 0.2.0.

*Early development version with automated tests, including every supported language. Live in-game validation, native-speaker translation review and visual checks in all languages are still pending. Restricted or unverified buff data is shown as unknown. Legacy perks are informational; selected ranks are checked in the game's menu. Quest projections use current reward values and may change with buffs, level or turn-in order. Historical AFK time cannot be reconstructed before tracking begins.*
